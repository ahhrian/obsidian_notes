Market Order -> Define quantity only, execution of trade guaranteed, no idea what price will be used

Limit Order -> Define both a price & quantity, no guarantee if execution takes place, must see if market matches the order proposed

Bracket Order (BO) -> Define both stop loss & take profit
Cover Order (CO) -> Define stop loss only

Intraday Short Selling Trades -> Can keep your short position over the day
- SGX does NOT allow Intraday Shorting
- Forex markets generally allow Intraday

Leverage
- Increase your purchasing power by "borrowing" money from broker
- Take when you have higher probability / confidence of winning



Golden Cross
- 50 Day Moving Avg. (MA) Crosses 200 Day MA (going up)
- Sentiment is that it'll keep upward moment for at least some time

Death Cross
- 50 Day MA Crosses 200 Day MA (going down)
- Likely will go down for some time

Most likely it will converge back to the 200 Day MA eventually

Efficient Market Hypothesis (EMH)
>The Market is perfectly randomly & therefore unpredictable???
- Weak Form (Not very strict): Technical Analysis cannot let you beat the market (historical data not reflective of future)
- Semi Strong: Technical Analysis + Fundamental Analysis together also cannot beat market
- Strong: No matter what (TA + FA + Insider Info) you can never beat the market

Behavioral Finance
- Trades/Humans are irrational

Adaptive Market Hypothesis (AMH)
- Market Efficiency evolves and changes as participants and environment change
